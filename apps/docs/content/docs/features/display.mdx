---
title: Display & scaling
description: Adjust YouTube Music zoom while keeping app controls readable.
---

![Display settings](/screenshots/display.png)

*Current UI captured in an isolated session with sample data.*

Open **Settings → Appearance → Display** and adjust **YouTube zoom** from **80% to 150%**, in 5% steps. Changes apply immediately to the YouTube Music view. Choose **Reset to 100%** to restore the default.

Toolbar, Settings, and tray view keep their normal size. Your operating system’s display scaling still applies to the app.

On a high-DPI display, use your preferred system scale. If the app feels sluggish or has rendering problems, check **Disable Hardware Acceleration** under **Generic**; changing it requires a restart.
