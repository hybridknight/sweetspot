Player mode gives playback its own window, separate from the full YouTube Music interface.

## Open the player

Click **Player mode** in the main window toolbar, or go to **Settings → Appearance → Player skins** and choose **Open player**.

## Choose a look

The built-in skins are **Spotlight**, **Studio**, **Aurora**, **Pop**, **Focus**, **Sleeve**, and **Classic**. Choose one in the gallery. Each skin remembers its own preferences.

The selected skin has a preview with sample content. Change its controls, then choose **Apply** to save or **Cancel** to discard. Apply or cancel pending edits before selecting another skin.

## Customize panels

Available options depend on the skin. They include colors and finishes, playlist spacing, artist names and durations, video, the equalizer panel, and a live spectrum. Hiding the equalizer panel does not disable audio processing. Audio-only tracks use artwork when video is unavailable.

Use the player controls to play, pause, seek, change tracks, shuffle, repeat, and change volume. Playlist controls support queue actions and reordering. Pin keeps the player above other windows; fullscreen gives the player more space.

Choose and customize the included skins in Settings. Importing and exporting skin packages is not available.

Player skins change the dedicated player. [Themes](/docs/features/themes) style the full YouTube Music view.
