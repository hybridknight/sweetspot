Open **Settings → Player → Equalizer**, or show the equalizer panel in player mode.

## Adjust your sound

1. Turn on the equalizer.
2. Choose a preset or adjust the ten frequency bands.
3. Leave automatic headroom enabled to reduce the level when boosts could clip.

The bands run from **31 Hz to 16 kHz**, with **−12 dB to +12 dB** gain. Low bands shape bass, middle bands affect vocals and instruments, and high bands adjust brightness. The response graph includes the automatic level reduction.

## Presets

Built-in presets are **Flat**, **Warm**, **Bass Boost**, **Bass Reduction**, **Vocal Clarity**, and **Gentle Treble**. Save a custom preset under a name you choose; manage it from the same panel.

The equalizer affects playback on this computer. Hiding the player’s equalizer panel keeps the current sound settings active. Disable the equalizer to bypass it.

[Consistent volume and playback fades](/docs/features/playback) have separate controls and can work with the equalizer disabled.
