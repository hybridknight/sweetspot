Click the Sweet Spot system tray or menu bar icon to open the compact now-playing popup.

Use playback controls, seek through the current track, like or dislike, and open the main window or Settings. Hover or drag the progress control to see a timestamp.

## Keep it available

Enable **Settings → Generic → Minimize to tray on close** to keep Sweet Spot running after closing its window.

Use **Pin** to keep the popup on top and open after clicking elsewhere. When pinned, drag the accent pill to move it. Otherwise, click away or press Escape to dismiss it.

For a separate customizable playback window with skins, playlist, and equalizer panels, use [Player mode](/docs/features/player).
