## Sound and transitions

| Setting | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Consistent volume | Gently reduces loud passages for more consistent local playback; enabled by default. |
| Smooth play and pause | Briefly fades sound in and out to soften starts and stops; enabled by default. |
| Song fades | Fades out at a song’s end and into the next song; off by default. |
| Song fade duration | Choose 0.5–5 seconds when song fades are enabled. |
| Exponential volume ratio | Gives the volume slider finer control at low levels. |

Song fades play songs sequentially. They do not overlap two songs, and loading time can still leave a gap. Sound processing applies on this computer; do not assume a Cast device uses the same processing.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Native media keys remain available. Enable **Global playback shortcuts** to control Sweet Spot while using other apps:

| Action | Windows | macOS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Previous track | Alt + Shift + Left | Option + Shift + Left |
| Next track | Alt + Shift + Right | Option + Shift + Right |
| Play / pause | Alt + Shift + Space | Option + Shift + Space |

Global shortcuts are disabled by default on macOS because the arrow combinations are also text-selection shortcuts. Changes apply immediately.

## Other playback options

- **Skip disliked songs** is experimental and skips a disliked track when the track changes.
- **Choose video resolution** lets you request a preferred video resolution. Availability depends on the track and YouTube Music.
- **Enable Taskbar Progress** shows playback progress where the platform supports it.
- **Enable Chromecast** is experimental. Restart after enabling it, and allow local-network discovery through your firewall. Devices must be reachable on your network.

Shared-link preferences are in the same page; see [Shared links](/docs/features/shared-links).

## Watch music videos

For tracks with an available music video, choose **Video** in the player to watch without returning to the full YouTube Music window. Choose **Artwork** to return to the cover view. The video panel is available in the player skins and can be shown or hidden in the skin’s panel settings.

Set your preferred resolution under **Settings → Playback → Video → Choose video resolution**. Available video quality depends on the track and YouTube Music. Audio-only tracks continue to show artwork.
