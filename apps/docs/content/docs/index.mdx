Sweet Spot brings YouTube Music to your desktop with a dedicated player, customizable skins, sound controls, synced lyrics, and optional AI assistant connections through MCP.

## Start listening

1. [Install Sweet Spot](/docs/install) for your computer.
2. [Sign in to YouTube Music](/docs/sign-in) using the separate browser window, or the in-app fallback.
3. Open **Settings** to choose your sound and appearance.

## Your music, in the conversation

Connect a desktop AI assistant through **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** to keep your music close while you work. Try “What’s playing in Sweet Spot?” or “What’s coming up in my queue?” Enable playback control to ask it to pause, skip a song, change volume, or queue a song from a YouTube link.

Start at **Settings → AI & MCP**. The guided setup helps you create a client and copy its connection config. The server is off by default, runs only on your computer, and requires a client token. Assistants have read-only access until you allow playback control. Sweet Spot must stay open, and music search is not available.

## Find a setting

| In Settings | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| Generic | Startup, close-to-tray, hardware acceleration, updates |
| Player → General | Playback, shortcuts, shared links, video preferences |
| Player → Equalizer | Frequency bands, presets, automatic headroom |
| Player → Lyrics | Timed lyrics, providers, highlighting |
| Appearance → Player skins | Skin gallery, customization, import/export |
| Appearance → Themes | Styles for the full YouTube Music view |
| Appearance → Display | YouTube Music zoom |
| AI & MCP | AI assistant setup, local MCP server, optional playback control |
| Local API | HTTP server and port |
| Access & pairing | Client tokens and revoking access |

Sweet Spot uses YouTube Music for your library and playback. Account availability and music access follow YouTube Music. Experimental Chromecast support is described in [playback](/docs/features/playback).
